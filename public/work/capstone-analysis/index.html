Capstone: Housing Price Analysis

Senior capstone project analyzing drivers of residential housing prices in the Northeast US.

December 1, 2025
RStatisticsRegression

Abstract

For my senior capstone, I investigated the primary drivers of residential housing prices across six Northeastern states using a dataset of 50,000+ home sales from 2019–2024. The analysis combined traditional hedonic pricing models with modern regularization techniques.

Methodology

  • Collected and cleaned data from multiple MLS sources
  • Applied LASSO and Ridge regression to identify the most predictive features
  • Built interactive visualizations using ggplot2 and Shiny
  • Validated findings against Zillow’s Zestimate model

Findings

School district quality and walkability score emerged as the two strongest predictors after controlling for square footage and location, explaining an additional 12% of variance beyond physical property characteristics alone.
