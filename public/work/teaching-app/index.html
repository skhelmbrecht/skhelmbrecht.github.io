Calculus Practice App

A web app generating randomized calculus practice problems with step-by-step solutions.

June 15, 2025
ReactLaTeXEducation

Motivation

While teaching Calculus I at NYU, I noticed students needed more practice problems with worked solutions than the textbook provided. I built a simple web app that generates unlimited practice problems for each topic.

Features

  • Randomized problem generation for limits, derivatives, and integrals
  • Step-by-step solutions rendered with KaTeX
  • Topic selection and difficulty levels
  • Progress tracking with local storage

Stack

React frontend with a lightweight problem-generation engine. Problems are defined as templates with randomized coefficients, ensuring students always get fresh practice material.
